Problem Statement

Sri Lankan Property Market Vulnerabilities

⚠️

1. Rampant Title Deed Fraud & Bogus Listings

Unregistered middlemen post unverified properties. Buyers risk life savings on properties burdened by undisclosed encumbrances, defective partition deeds, or local council boundary violations.

⚖️

2. Absence of Pre-Listing Legal Screening

Traditional portals act as passive bulletin boards. Real estate attorneys are only contacted after purchase disputes arise, causing months of costly litigation.

👥

3. Rigid Multi-Account Registration Friction

Conventional web architectures tie users permanently to a single role. A registered buyer wishing to sell property is blocked by unique email constraints, forcing separate accounts.

Major Innovation

Unified Single-Account Dynamic Role Switching

Legacy Multi-Account Problem

  • • Rigid role enum ('buyer', 'seller', 'lawyer') locked 1 user to 1 fixed role.
  • • When a buyer visited 'Sell', redirected to registration — which failed due to duplicate email database constraint.
  • • Lawyers were trapped inside LawyerDashboard with hidden navbars, unable to browse or bookmark properties.

Newly Engineered Unified Architecture

  • • Decoupled selling privileges into a seller_status enum ('none', 'pending', 'active').
  • • Developed in-place POST /api/user/apply-seller endpoint allowing buyers to apply without logging out.
  • • Dynamic 1-click Navbar Mode Switcher pills (🛒 Buyer, 🏷️ Seller, ⚖️ Lawyer).

Problem Solving

Key Engineering Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1: Duplicate Email Collision

Solution: Decoupled seller rights from authentication via seller_status column and in-place applyForSeller API.

Challenge 2: Lawyer Workspace Isolation

Solution: Built bidirectional cross-navigation in Navbar and dashboards, allowing lawyers to browse as buyers freely.

Challenge 3: Document Privacy & Leaks

Solution: Isolated NIC files and deeds in private storage disk with authenticated administrative proxy routes.

Challenge 4: Client State Desynchronization

Solution: Implemented refreshUser() in AuthContext to synchronize React state with localStorage on role changes.
